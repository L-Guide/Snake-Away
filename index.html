How to Play

🐍

Each snake has an arrow showing which way it points. Tap a snake to send it sliding off the board!

✅

A snake can only exit if its arrow path is completely clear — no other snakes blocking.

⏰

Every level has a timer — clear all snakes before time runs out! Watch the timer bar carefully.

💔

Tapping a blocked or locked snake costs a heart. You get 3 hearts per level — lose them all and you must retry.

🧊

Icy snakes (frosted look) need 2 taps — one to crack the ice, one more to send it off.

🔒

Locked snakes (lock icon) can't move until the snake holding their key (key icon) is cleared first. Look for the golden line connecting them!

🎁

Treasure boxes appear on some levels. Clear a snake next to them to collect the prize inside!

🔄

Use Undo to take back your last move, or Hint to get help!

🎬

Watch an ad to get extra time when the timer runs out, or revive with 3 hearts when you lose them all!

🏆

Clear every snake to complete the level. Fewer mistakes earn more stars — up to 3 per level!
